# data/

Put your clips here. They are git-ignored, so raw, unblurred footage never gets committed.

- `oblique45.mp4`: phone at about 45° to the athlete, whole body in frame, 2–3 m away
- `front.mp4`: optional, phone straight in front

Anyone else in the frame is blurred in every shared video (`src/privacy_blur.py`), but only film adults who have agreed to it.
